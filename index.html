Our Story

Pizza Toppers was born from a single obsession: the perfect wood-fired pizza. Our 900°F stone oven, imported from Naples, chars every base to crispy perfection while keeping the centre soft and airy. Every ingredient is sourced fresh daily — San Marzano tomatoes, buffalo mozzarella, hand-picked basil. This isn't fast food. This is craft.
